## Idolatry as a Form of Sexual Immorality  
The Bible presents idolatry as a form of sexual immorality, linking it to covetousness and other vices. It warns that idolatry leads to God’s wrath and excludes individuals from the Kingdom of God. This connection is made explicit in several passages, emphasizing that idolatry is a moral failing that separates people from divine favor. [P1] [P3] [P11]  

## Idolatry and the Consequences of Sin  
The Bible describes idolatry as a serious sin that results in divine judgment and spiritual defilement. It portrays idolatry as a rejection of God’s authority and a cause for the destruction of nations and individuals. The text also warns that idolatry leads to moral corruption and the loss of divine blessings. [P2] [P7] [P10] [P12]  

## Idolatry as a Rejection of God’s Authority  
Idolatry is consistently depicted as a rejection of God’s sovereignty and a defiance of His commandments. The Bible emphasizes that idolatry involves the worship of created things rather than the Creator, which is a fundamental act of rebellion. This theme is reinforced through historical and prophetic passages that highlight the consequences of idolatry for both individuals and communities. [P13] [P15] [P16] [P20]  

## Idolatry and the Call to Holiness  
The Bible calls believers to reject idolatry and to pursue holiness, emphasizing that idolatry is incompatible with a life of righteousness. It warns against the dangers of idolatry and encourages the pursuit of a relationship with God through obedience and faith. The text also highlights the importance of community in resisting idolatry and maintaining spiritual purity. [P14] [P17] [P21] [P25]
